The ZHL Productivity Pack is a Chrome extension for Zillow Home Loans staff that bundles 30+ workflow shortcuts across Gmail, Salesforce, the Loan Officer Portal, and Genesys. Every module can be turned on or off from the extension's Setup page; the pack ships with sensible defaults and a friendly walkthrough.

Highlights

• Appraisal Blast — when a Reggora "Appraisal Submission Summary" email arrives, a single "Send to all parties" button parses the loan, pulls every Contact Role from Salesforce, opens a Gmail draft pre-addressed to the Borrower / Co-Borrower / Buyer's Agent / Transaction Coordinator (CC: Processor), forwards the appraisal PDF, and inserts a branded HTML body — congratulatory or low-value variant depending on the appraised vs. purchase price.

• Meeting Reminders — Outlook-style pop-up reminders inside Gmail, sourced from your Google Calendar. 30-minute and 5-minute lead times by default (configurable), Snooze with event-relative options ("1 min before event", "at start time", etc.), Dismiss, draggable + resizable, brings Gmail to the front when a reminder fires. Catches Zoom and Google Meet links automatically so a 📹 Join button appears right on the card.

• Loan Comparison PDF — borrower-facing comparison handout for the Pricing & Scenarios page. Prints every selected scenario with full PITIA breakdown (P&I, MI, taxes, insurance, HOA), itemized closing costs, and a 2% Grant variant. Includes a "Print Buyer Worksheet" companion for the rate-options table.

• VPA Email / Pre-Approval / Insurance Intro — one-click branded emails composed from Salesforce data, with editable templates in Setup.

• Pricing Exception Workflow — a branched modal that walks the LO through the locked vs. unlocked PE submission steps, auto-computes PE dollars and points, and hands you a ready-to-send email at the end.

• DTI Max Estimator — inline pill on each pricing-results row showing the maximum purchase price that fits the program's back-end DTI cap, given the borrower's income and liabilities.

• 2-1 Buydown calculator, VA Residual Income calculator, Net Proceeds calculator, Zoho Booking auto-paster, Caller ID for Genesys + Gmail, Drag Gmail attachments anywhere, Bulk task delete/download in LOP, FHA Collections + Disputed badges, FHA Flip-Rule + Manual-eligible warnings, Co-borrower → Salesforce, Loan Story generator, Loan Amount field for LOP, Scenario Sort, Address Copy, SMS Mark-All-Read, SMS Add Participants, Update Toast, Auto-Call-Details / Auto-Messaging tab, Salesforce Contact SMS quick-open, Salesforce promotions auto-hide, Gmail layout fixes.

How it stays out of your way

• Every module has a kill-switch in Setup. Disable any you don't want.
• A global kill-switch instantly disables every module across every tab.
• Nothing leaves your browser unless you explicitly click a button that opens a Gmail draft, opens a Salesforce tab, etc. No telemetry beyond an opt-out "time saved" tally local to your Chrome profile.
• Stored locally in chrome.storage.local on your device only — never synced to the cloud.

Onboarding

The first time you load the extension, the setup page opens automatically. A guided walkthrough page lists every module with a screenshot and a one-line "what it does." Click the toolbar icon any time to revisit it.

Email Attachments Action Needed

Two PDFs are automatically attached to every VPA email. The fields below are pre-filled with Justin's documents — you need to replace them with your own or the attachments will not work for your account.

⚠ Important: The default file IDs belong to Justin's Google Drive. You must upload your own versions of these PDFs to your Google Drive and replace the IDs below, otherwise the attachments will fail.
💡 How to find a Google Drive File ID:
  1. Open Google Drive in your browser
  2. Find the PDF file you want to attach
  3. Right-click the file, then select Open with → Open in new tab (double-click won't show the URL)
  4. Look at the URL in your browser's address bar. It will look like this:
    https://drive.google.com/file/d/1AbCdEfGhIjKlMnOpQrStUvWxYz/view
  5. Copy the part between /d/ and /view — that's the File ID
  6. Paste it into the field below
